## Injected messiness — the QA answer key
Run your mod_Reconcile / mod_DataQuality logic against this and check your counts match. If they don't, something's being silently dropped.

| Issue | Count | Where | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duplicate Invoice ID (same ID, different Amount) | 18 | invoices_raw | A naive lookup double-counts these unless MatchLog flags the duplicate before reconciling |
| Orphaned payment (Invoice ID matches nothing) | 66 | payments_raw | Your Dictionary build pass needs to log these, not silently skip them |
| Blank Customer Name | 30 | invoices_raw | Breaks customer-level grouping/pivots unless handled |
| Blank Region | 45 | invoices_raw | Same issue, for region-level KPIs |
| Invoice ID with leading/trailing whitespace | 45 | invoices_raw | Exact-string Dictionary lookups fail on these unless you `Trim()` during cleaning |
| Payment Date in `MM/DD/YYYY` instead of `YYYY-MM-DD` | 380 | payments_raw | A blind `CDate()` will misparse some of these unless both formats are handled explicitly |
